Kiwix is a nonprofit organisation making free knowledge accessible where the Internet is not.

At Kiwix, we believe access to knowledge is a fundamental right, which is why we’re dedicated to providing free and open access to everyone, everywhere.

We create and support open technologies that bring the world’s knowledge Offline via our own open-source software dedicated to providing offline access to free educational content, and more…

Why does Offline access matter?

Because 50% of the world’s population lacks reliable internet access due to cost, infrastructure, or censorship. At Kiwix, we find this unacceptable, so our challenge is to bridge the digital divide and provide access to knowledge for as many people as possible worldwide.
